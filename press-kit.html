About

About the app

Swiss Naturalization Test is a free study companion for the Swiss citizenship exam, built by Vincenzo Casini — an Italian software engineer living in Morges — to prepare for his own naturalization exam, then made free for every candidate across Switzerland.

Free, ad-free, offline, no account, no data collection. Your commune in all 26 cantons, with honest per-canton exam preparation.
