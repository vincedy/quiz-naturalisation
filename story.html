Our story

From a family quiz to all of Switzerland

An app born at home to revise for naturalisation, now a tool for all 26 cantons. The journey, step by step.

  8. 10.06
    From 8 to 11 cantons.

    The first 8: VD, GE, NE, VS, BE, ZH, AG, LU. The canton publishes its official questions: the app uses those.

    The 3 new ones: Fribourg (official civics programme · oral exam), St. Gallen (study material for the official test), Ticino (240 questions in Italian, from official and public sources). The canton publishes no questions: the app writes them from its official material. The first with this method.

    From 11 to 26 cantons.

    The 15 still missing: UR, SZ, OW, NW, GL, ZG, SO, BS, BL, SH, AR, AI, GR, TG, JU. No cantonal questions yet: from day one the official federal base, your own commune to pick and, for each canton, a sheet saying what the app offers and how faithfully.
